## M14 Multi-business: plan (written before the code)

Design: a **registry** (`src/db/registry.js`) holds businesses, owners and one complete in-memory repo set per business. Every row carries `businessId`; ids come from shared per-table counters, so an id from business B simply does not exist in business A (404, not "forbidden"). Services keep taking `repos`, so they cannot reach another business's data by construction. Routes resolve the business from the session (owner) or from `:slug` (customer) and never from a parameter the owner controls; per-business routers are cached.

Steps (one commit each, `npm test` green at each):
1. Data: registry, scoped tables, seed as the `mehfil-kitchen` business, demo owner from `OWNER_EMAIL` and the existing hash. Existing tests adapted.
2. Auth: `/signup` (starter template, bcrypt 12, honeypot, per-IP limit, cap 50), `/owner/login` by email (generic error), session = `ownerId` + `businessId`, owner routes per business.
3. Public URLs: `/b/:slug/enquire`, `/b/:slug/api/*`, `/b/:slug/q/:token`, `/enquire` redirect, share card and QR, signed links carry `businessId`, landing buttons.
4. Budget: per-business caps (lower for new businesses), `AI_GLOBAL_DAILY_CAP`, friendly fallback, usage in Setup, owner email for notices.
5. Tests (isolation for every owner route, signup, login, slugs, caps, seed unchanged), openapi, PRD next steps, report.
